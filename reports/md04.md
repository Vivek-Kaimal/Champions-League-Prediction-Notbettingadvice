- 5 Nov: bets 9 and 12 cashed out at $2,198.53 (+$698.53) and $1,515.79 (+$15.79). Napoli's 1–4 left them on 3 pts, and Como's 9–1 raised Como's top-8 risk, so both bets lost value despite three wins.

**Round 4 result:** +$639.32. **Realised to date: +$1,848.16** (62% of target). No capital lost; no open bets.

**Lessons:**
- I was too confident in holding to the end. In football every result moves the table, there are too many teams, and their results are linked, so no forecast is accurate enough to rely on. We cannot predict; we can only forecast with probability.
- Both bets peaked on 22 Oct at a combined $4,540 (+$1,540). Cashing out then would have banked about $825 more. Profit should be taken when it appears.
- I was thinking like a stock-market investor, holding for the long run. This is trading, closer to futures: every bet has a fixed expiry (28 Jan), so time is limited. Set the target when the bet is placed and exit at it, whatever happens.
- New rules from here: **take profit at +20% above stake**, and **pots of two teams from MD5**. With four matchdays left there is much more information but less time to recover from a bad result.
