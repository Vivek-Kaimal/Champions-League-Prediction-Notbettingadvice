The thesis

Why 9th–24th, and how positions are managed.

  1. Top 8 is unpredictable: in each of the last two league phases, about three of the eight places went to teams nobody expected.
  2. Bottom 12 is mostly predictable, apart from one to three overperformers.
  3. So the edge is in the middle. 9th–24th is 16 of 36 places. Predict the edges correctly and the middle falls into place.

Rolling strategy: each bet is all-or-nothing, so positions are cashed out once in profit and risk rises, then rebuilt after each matchday on better information. Capital first; lower returns are the accepted cost.
